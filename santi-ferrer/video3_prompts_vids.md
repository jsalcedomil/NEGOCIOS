# Video 3: "Mide tu nivel hoy" (Serie "Rumbo a la convocatoria", día 1), prompts para Google Vids

**Duración final estimada:** 48–52 s · **8 escenas** · Vertical 9:16

## Configuración general en Vids

- **Ingredientes:** la foto oficial de Santi (`video1_inicio_hd.jpg`).
- **Formato:** Vertical (Portrait).
- **Cómo pegar el prompt:** en cada escena pega primero el **BLOQUE BASE** y, debajo, el **bloque de la escena**. Son dos bloques en el mismo cuadro.

## BLOQUE BASE (va en todas las escenas)

```
Realistic UGC video, vertical portrait format. Use the man from the reference image exactly as he is: same face, same short black hair with faded sides and the front combed to one side, same light stubble, same small scar on his left eyebrow, black smartwatch, green paracord bracelet. Today he wears a plain sand-colored fitted t-shirt and black training shorts, running shoes (no backpack). Location: a red athletics running track in a stadium in Cochabamba, Bolivia, early morning soft sunlight, empty stands and mountains in the background. Natural lip sync. He says each line only once and never repeats a phrase. If the video is a selfie, the phone recording it is never visible in the frame. Ambient outdoor sound only. No subtitles, no captions, no text on screen, no music, no logos.
```

## ESCENA 1: gancho (6 s)

```
Selfie at arm's length on the track. He looks focused into the camera and presses the button on his black smartwatch as if starting a stopwatch. Dialogue, in Spanish with a warm Bolivian Latin American accent, energetic: "Hoy vas a saber exactamente cuánto te falta para aprobar." He starts speaking at the first second.
```

## ESCENA 2: prueba 1, barras (7 s)

```
The phone is now on a small tripod at chest height, filming him full body next to a metal pull-up bar beside the track. He looks at the camera and says in Spanish with a warm Bolivian accent: "Prueba uno: barras. El máximo que puedas… sin trampa." Then he jumps up, grabs the bar and does two strict full-range pull-ups, chin over the bar, full extension at the bottom.
```

## ESCENA 3: prueba 2, flexiones (6 s)

```
Phone on a small tripod low to the ground, side-front angle, filming him full body on the red track. He kneels, looks at the camera and says in Spanish with a warm Bolivian accent: "Prueba dos: flexiones en un minuto." Then he gets into position and does fast, strict push-ups: body straight like a plank, chest close to the ground.
```

## ESCENA 4: prueba 3, abdominales (6 s)

```
Phone on a small tripod low to the ground, filming him on a black exercise mat on the grass next to the track. He sits on the mat, looks at the camera and says in Spanish with a warm Bolivian accent: "Prueba tres: abdominales en un minuto." Then he lies back and does fast sit-ups with his hands behind his head.
```

## ESCENA 5: prueba 4, carrera (10 s)

```
Selfie at arm's length on the track, he taps his black smartwatch while talking. Dialogue, in Spanish with a warm Bolivian accent, clear and energetic: "Prueba cuatro: corré la distancia que pide tu convocatoria y anotá tu tiempo. Yo uso mi reloj… el celular también sirve." After finishing the sentence he smiles and starts jogging forward along the track.
```

## ESCENA 6: anotá y pegalo (8 s)

```
Selfie at arm's length. He is sitting on the red track after the tests, sweaty and breathing a bit hard, holding a small notebook with a pen (pages blank or unreadable, no visible text). Dialogue, in Spanish with a warm Bolivian accent, motivating tone: "Anotá todo en un papel y pegalo en tu pared. En treinta días repetimos y comparamos."
```

## ESCENA 7: comentá tus números (6 s)

```
Selfie at arm's length, still sitting on the track, big friendly smile. On "comentarios" he points down with his index finger (to the comments). Dialogue, in Spanish with a warm Bolivian accent, cheerful: "Dejame tus números en los comentarios… voy a responder a varios."
```

## ESCENA 8: cierre en la pista (8 s)

```
Selfie at arm's length, standing on the track with the stadium behind him. His expression changes from friendly to serious, firm and determined. He stands tall, and on "¡Firmes y darle!" he places his right fist firmly on his chest. Then he says the second sentence slowly and with conviction, with a short pause after "Grandes logros". At the end he gives one slow nod and holds a steady gaze into the camera for one second. Dialogue, in Spanish with a warm Bolivian accent, strong motivational tone: "¡Firmes y darle! Grandes logros… requieren grandes sacrificios."
```

## Revisa antes de exportar

- Lleva **polera color arena, short negro y está en la pista roja** en todas las escenas.
- En las escenas 2, 3 y 4 hace el ejercicio **bien hecho** (barras completas, flexiones con el cuerpo recto).
- **No repite frases** y **no se ve el celular** que graba.
- Dice **"corré"**, **"anotá"**, **"pegalo"** y **"Dejame"** (voseo).
- Exporta todas las escenas juntas en un solo MP4 (o en dos o tres partes). Yo las separo.
