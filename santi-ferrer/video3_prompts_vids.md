# Video 3: "Flexiones y barras: así te evalúan en el COLMIL", prompts para Google Vids

**Basado en:** el video oficial "Proceso de admisión 2024–2025 – Pruebas físicas" (ver `pruebas_fisicas_colmil_2024-2025.md`).
**Duración final estimada:** 45–50 s · **6 escenas** · Vertical 9:16

## Configuración en Vids

- **Ingredientes:** la foto oficial de Santi.
- **Formato:** Vertical.
- **Cómo pegar el prompt:** pega primero el **BLOQUE BASE** y, debajo, el **bloque de la escena**. Son dos bloques en el mismo cuadro.

## BLOQUE BASE

```
Realistic UGC video, vertical portrait format. Use the man from the reference image exactly as he is: same face, same short black hair with faded sides and the front combed to one side, same light stubble, same small scar on his left eyebrow, black smartwatch, green paracord bracelet. Today he wears a plain white fitted t-shirt and black training shorts, white socks and running shoes (no backpack). Location: a red athletics running track in a stadium in Cochabamba, Bolivia, sunny morning, mountains in the background. Natural lip sync. He says each line only once and never repeats a phrase. If the video is a selfie, the phone recording it is never visible in the frame. Ambient outdoor sound only. No subtitles, no captions, no text on screen, no music, no logos, no military uniform.
```

## ESCENA 1: gancho (6 s)

```
Selfie at arm's length on the track, serious and direct, slightly leaning toward the camera. Dialogue, in Spanish with a warm Bolivian Latin American accent, firm tone: "Así te evalúan flexiones y barras en el COLMIL… y por esto te anulan repeticiones." He starts speaking at the first second.
```

## ESCENA 2: flexiones, técnica (8 s)

```
The phone is on a small tripod low to the ground, side-front angle, filming him full body on a blue foam mat on the track. Under his chest there is a small black wedge-shaped contact pad. He is already in push-up position: arms fully extended, hands exactly shoulder-width apart, body perfectly straight from head to heels. While doing slow, perfect push-ups where his chest touches the black pad at the bottom and his arms fully extend at the top, he says in Spanish with a warm Bolivian accent: "Flexiones: brazos estirados, manos a la altura de los hombros… y el cuerpo recto todo el tiempo."
```

## ESCENA 3: flexiones, marca y errores (10 s)

```
Selfie at arm's length, he is kneeling next to the blue mat, counting on his fingers while he talks, clear and serious tone. Dialogue, in Spanish with a warm Bolivian accent: "Varones: sesenta y siete en dos minutos. Damas: cincuenta y tres. No vale si levantás la cadera, si el pecho no toca el aparato o si apoyás la rodilla."
```

## ESCENA 4: barras, técnica (8 s)

```
The phone is on a small tripod at chest height, filming him full body hanging from an outdoor metal pull-up bar next to the track, overhand grip (palms facing away from him), hands shoulder-width apart, arms fully extended, legs still and straight. He does two slow, strict pull-ups bringing his chin clearly above the bar and lowering until his arms are completely straight, body without swinging. Then he drops down, looks at the camera and says in Spanish with a warm Bolivian accent: "Barras: agarre prono, manos al ancho de hombros… y el mentón tiene que pasar la barra."
```

## ESCENA 5: barras, marca y errores (10 s)

```
Selfie at arm's length, standing under the pull-up bar, he points up at the bar while talking, firm tone. Dialogue, in Spanish with a warm Bolivian accent: "Varones: seis repeticiones. Damas: cuatro. No vale si te balanceás, si pataleás o si no estirás completo los brazos."
```

## ESCENA 6: cierre (8 s)

```
Selfie at arm's length, standing on the track. His expression changes from friendly to serious, firm and determined. He stands tall, and on "¡Firmes y darle!" he places his right fist firmly on his chest. Then he says the second sentence slowly and with conviction, with a short pause after "Grandes logros". At the end he gives one slow nod and holds a steady gaze into the camera for one second. Dialogue, in Spanish with a warm Bolivian accent, strong motivational tone: "¡Firmes y darle! Grandes logros… requieren grandes sacrificios."
```

## Revisa antes de exportar

- La técnica tiene que verse **perfecta**: cuerpo recto, el pecho toca el pad, el mentón pasa la barra y los brazos se estiran completos. Si sale mal, regenera esa escena.
- El agarre en barras es **prono**: palmas hacia adelante, no hacia la cara.
- Dice bien los números: **"sesenta y siete"**, **"cincuenta y tres"**, **"seis"** y **"cuatro"**.
- **No repite frases** y **no se ve el celular**.
