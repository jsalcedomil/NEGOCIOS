# Video 2: "El error #1 del postulante", prompts para Google Vids

**Duración final estimada:** 45–48 s · **7 escenas** · Vertical 9:16

## Configuración general en Vids

- **Ingredientes:**
  1. La foto oficial de Santi (la de las gradas, `video1_inicio_hd.jpg`), para mantener la cara.
  2. Opcional: la imagen del parque con barras (`video2_inicio`, en Canva: https://www.canva.com/M/MAHZE_y9QI4).
- **Formato:** Vertical (Portrait).
- **Cómo pegar el prompt:** en cada escena pega primero el **BLOQUE BASE** y, debajo, el **bloque de la escena**.

## BLOQUE BASE (va en todas las escenas)

```
Realistic UGC selfie video, handheld smartphone at arm's length, vertical portrait format. Use the man from the reference image exactly as he is: same face, same short black hair with faded sides and the front combed to one side, same light stubble, same small scar on his left eyebrow, black smartwatch, green paracord bracelet. Today he wears a plain black fitted t-shirt and black joggers (no backpack). Location: an outdoor public park in Cochabamba, Bolivia, with metal calisthenics pull-up bars behind him, trees and mountains in the background, bright mid-morning sunlight. Natural lip sync, ambient park sound only (birds, distant city). He starts speaking at the first second. No subtitles, no captions, no text on screen, no music, no logos.
```

## ESCENA 1: gancho (8 s)

```
He looks serious and direct into the camera, raises his free hand with the index finger up as a warning. Dialogue, in Spanish with a warm Bolivian Latin American accent, firm and intriguing tone: "Muchos postulantes reprueban la prueba física por esto… y no es por falta de fuerza." After finishing he keeps a serious look for a moment.
```

## ESCENA 2: el problema (8 s)

```
He shakes his head slightly with a disappointed half-smile, and with his free hand mimics running and then lifting a weight while he talks. Dialogue, in Spanish with a warm Bolivian accent: "Entrenan a ciegas. Corren, hacen pesas… pero nunca leyeron qué pruebas les van a tomar."
```

## ESCENA 3: por qué importa (8 s)

```
Medium close-up, camera a bit closer to his face. He counts on his fingers, emphatic and clear. Dialogue, in Spanish with a warm Bolivian accent: "Ni cuál es la marca mínima. Y cada instituto pide cosas distintas… y cambian cada convocatoria."
```

## ESCENA 4: me pasó a mí (8 s)

```
He gives a small self-ironic laugh and rubs the back of his head with his free hand, then looks back at the camera honestly. Dialogue, in Spanish with a warm Bolivian accent, sincere tone: "Yo cometí ese error: entrené meses para una prueba… que ni siquiera me iban a tomar."
```

## ESCENA 5: la tarea (10 s)

```
He is now sitting on a park bench next to the pull-up bars, holding the selfie phone with one hand and a small notebook and pen in the other. He lifts the notebook briefly toward the camera (pages blank or unreadable, no visible text). Clear, motivating "coach" tone. Dialogue, in Spanish with a warm Bolivian accent: "Tu tarea de hoy no es entrenar. Bajá la convocatoria oficial de tu instituto y anotá cada prueba y la marca que te piden."
```

## ESCENA 6: el objetivo (6 s)

```
Standing again in front of the pull-up bars, confident half-smile, he points at the camera with his index finger. Dialogue, in Spanish with a warm Bolivian accent, energetic: "Ese es tu objetivo. Mañana medimos dónde estás vos."
```

## ESCENA 7: cierre (8 s)

**Elegido: cierre nuevo en el parque.** Pegar el BLOQUE BASE + este bloque:

```
His expression changes from friendly to serious, firm and determined. He stands tall in front of the pull-up bars, and on "¡Firmes y darle!" he places his right fist firmly on his chest. Then he says the second sentence slowly and with conviction, with a short pause after "Grandes logros". At the end he gives one slow nod and holds a steady gaze into the camera for one second. Dialogue, in Spanish with a warm Bolivian accent, strong motivational tone: "¡Firmes y darle! Grandes logros… requieren grandes sacrificios." The phone recording the video is never visible in the frame; only his arm extends toward the camera.
```
