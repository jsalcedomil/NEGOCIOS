## ESCENA 1: gancho (6 s)

```
Selfie at arm's length on the track, energetic and direct. Dialogue, in Spanish with a warm Bolivian Latin American accent: "Parte dos: abdominales, aeróbica y salto alto… así te evalúan en el COLMIL." He starts speaking at the first second.
```

## ESCENA 2: abdominales, técnica (10 s)

```
The phone is on a small tripod low to the ground, side angle, filming him full body on a blue foam mat on the track. He holds a camouflage-pattern medicine ball with both arms extended. Start position: sitting, knees bent, feet flat on the mat, the ball touching the top of his feet. Then he lies back fully, body straight at 180 degrees, feet together, arms extended behind his head, and the ball touches the mat behind his head. He repeats the movement smoothly, always keeping arms straight and feet together. While doing it he says in Spanish with a warm Bolivian accent: "Abdominales con balón: tocás el empeine… te estirás completo… y el balón toca el suelo atrás."
```

## ESCENA 3: abdominales, marca y errores (10 s)

```
Selfie at arm's length, sitting on the blue mat holding the camouflage medicine ball on his lap, counting on his fingers, clear and serious tone. Dialogue, in Spanish with a warm Bolivian accent: "Varones: sesenta y siete en dos minutos. Damas: cincuenta y tres. No vale si el balón no toca el suelo o el empeine… o si separás los pies."
```

## ESCENA 4: prueba aeróbica (10 s)

```
Selfie at arm's length while he jogs steadily along the red running track, slightly out of breath but talking clearly, he glances at his black smartwatch. Dialogue, in Spanish with a warm Bolivian accent, motivating tone: "Aeróbica: tres mil doscientos metros. Varones, en trece minutos treinta y ocho. Damas, en quince minutos treinta y ocho."
```

## ESCENA 5: salto alto, técnica (8 s)

```
The phone is on a tripod at a wide side angle, filming a high-jump setup on the track: a horizontal bar on two white stands at about waist height and a thick red landing mat behind it. He stands at the start, looks at the camera and says in Spanish with a warm Bolivian accent: "Salto alto: un metro varones, noventa y cinco centímetros damas. Tijera o Fosbury." Then he takes a short curved run-up and clears the bar with a clean scissors jump, landing on the red mat without touching the bar.
```

## ESCENA 6: salto alto, advertencia (8 s)

```
Selfie at arm's length, standing next to the high-jump bar and the red mat, serious warning expression, he points at the bar. Dialogue, in Spanish with a warm Bolivian accent, firm tone: "Ojo: es prueba excluyente. Si tirás la barra… quedás fuera del proceso."
```

## ESCENA 7: cierre (8 s)

```
Selfie at arm's length, standing on the track. His expression changes from friendly to serious, firm and determined. He stands tall, and on "¡Firmes y darle!" he places his right fist firmly on his chest. Then he says the second sentence slowly and with conviction, with a short pause after "Grandes logros". At the end he gives one slow nod and holds a steady gaze into the camera for one second. Dialogue, in Spanish with a warm Bolivian accent, strong motivational tone: "¡Firmes y darle! Grandes logros… requieren grandes sacrificios."
```

> **Para ahorrar créditos:** puedes reutilizar el cierre del video 3 (misma ropa y misma pista).

## Revisa antes de exportar

- **Abdominales:** el balón toca **el suelo atrás** y **el empeine adelante**, y los pies van juntos y los brazos estirados.
- **Salto alto:** salta con **técnica de tijera** y **no toca la barra**. Es la escena más difícil para la IA: genera 3 o 4 versiones.
- Dice bien los números: **"sesenta y siete"**, **"cincuenta y tres"**, **"tres mil doscientos"**, **"trece minutos treinta y ocho"**, **"quince minutos treinta y ocho"**, **"un metro"** y **"noventa y cinco centímetros"**.
- **No repite frases** y **no se ve el celular**.
